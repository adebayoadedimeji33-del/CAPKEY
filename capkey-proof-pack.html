The pitch

"Every AI agent needs money to take actions. Today the only way to give it spending ability is to hand over your entire wallet — or approve every transaction manually, which defeats the point of autonomy. CAPKEY creates an onchain allowance instead. Deposit USDC into a Solana vault, set simple limits: per-transaction cap, daily cap, approved recipients, expiry. The agent can only spend within those bounds. Even if it's compromised or prompt-injected, a payment that violates policy is rejected by the chain itself — not by a client-side check that a bad actor could skip. One policy, enforced onchain, zero keys handed over."

Why it's different from a plain token delegate

  • SPL approve gives a delegate one number — a spending cap, nothing else.
  • CAPKEY enforces a full policy in one instruction: per-tx cap + daily cap (auto-resets) + recipient allowlist + expiry + an instant, permanent kill switch — with leftover funds recoverable by the owner after revoke.
